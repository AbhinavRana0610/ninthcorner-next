About the Umesh Residence

The Umesh Residence in Bhiwani, Haryana, is an elegant family home that combines soft creams, warm wood, brass accents and touches of sage green. The design covers several bedrooms, a formal living room, a dining area and a curved, contemporary façade.

Bedrooms

The bedrooms share a refined language of fluted panels, backlit arched niches and wooden slats. In the master bedroom, a padded headboard sits against a panel of vertical arches lit from within, beside mirrored wardrobes and a dressing table. Another room features a classic buttoned headboard framed by floral wallpaper panels and wall mouldings, with crystal pendants for a softer, more traditional feel. A third bedroom uses an upholstered bed in muted blue-grey with full-height wardrobes and a wall-mounted TV unit trimmed in brass.

Living room

The living room is designed around large carved rose-motif panels set into timber frames, with sage-green and cream sofas arranged on a marble-patterned rug. Crystal chandeliers, round smoked-glass coffee tables and a textured stone partition add layers of light and reflection.

Dining

The dining area features a statement wall of stone and timber tiles in a woven pattern, with a cream sideboard, upholstered chairs and a wave-shaped crystal light above the table. Together they make the dining area feel like a destination of its own.

Façade

Outside, curved white balconies with timber soffits, planters and vertical wood cladding give the house a flowing, modern elevation that stands out at dusk.

Design approach

The design blends classic and contemporary details so the home feels elegant but not old-fashioned. Fluted panels, arches and brass trims give structure, while sage green, cream and soft florals keep the rooms gentle and welcoming for the whole family.

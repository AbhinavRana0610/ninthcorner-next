Our Story

9th Corner, registered as Ninth Corner Dream Designs Private Limited, is an interior design firm that has been designing homes, workplaces and commercial spaces for 22 years. In that time we have delivered more than 800 spaces, from compact city apartments to villas, farmhouses, offices, showrooms and salons. Today we are a full-service interior design firm with our head office in Pitampura, Delhi, and a branch in Gurgaon.

Our approach is to capture your lifestyle and turn it into a design that is really you. We combine creativity with functionality, so the spaces we create are as practical to live and work in as they are beautiful to look at.

A Word From Our Director

Our director, Rahul Gupta, leads the company. His belief is simple: design is about shaping experiences, not just filling rooms. Every project is a collaboration with the client, and every space should tell the story of the people who use it. That conviction guides how our whole team works, from the first conversation to the final handover.

How We Work

Every project follows the same disciplined sequence: a detailed brief, space planning, concept design, photorealistic 3D visualisation, working drawings, material selection, procurement and supervised execution. Clients see their space before it is built, and changes are made on screen rather than on site. Our pricing is transparent and milestone-based with no hidden costs. Timelines are fixed, and our work is backed by a 10-year warranty.

Why Clients Choose an Integrated Interior Design Firm

Working with separate architects, designers and contractors often means gaps between drawings and reality, and nobody fully responsible when something goes wrong. Choosing one interior design firm for planning, design and execution gives you a single team, a single schedule and a single point of accountability from the first meeting to the final handover.

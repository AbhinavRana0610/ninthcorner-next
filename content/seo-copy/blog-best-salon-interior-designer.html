How 9th Corner Approaches Salon Projects

At 9th Corner, we treat every salon as a business first and a beautiful space second. Before we draw anything, we ask how your salon makes money: quick, high-volume services, long colour appointments, spa treatments or retail sales. That answer decides the number of stations, the size of the wash area, the number of treatment rooms and how much space reception needs. As a salon interior designer, we then plan the client journey from the door to the chair and back to the payment counter, keeping routes short and private where they need to be.

Mirror lighting gets special attention because it affects colour accuracy and how clients feel about the result. Finishes are chosen to withstand water, chemicals and constant use.

Every project includes photorealistic 3D views, so you can see the finished salon before work begins. Our pricing is transparent and milestone-based with no hidden costs, timelines are fixed so you can plan your launch, and our work is backed by a 10-year warranty.

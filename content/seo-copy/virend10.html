About the Virendra Residence

The Virendra Residence in Gurgaon is a glamorous home that blends classic architecture with luxurious, contemporary interiors. Soft creams, polished marble, gold accents and rich burgundy highlights run through the living spaces, staircase, pooja room, bedrooms and the elegant façade.

Living room

The formal living room is framed by a layered ceiling with cove lighting and crystal chandeliers. Curved cream sofas with burgundy cushions, gold side tables, sculptural accessories and a backlit textured feature wall create a rich, welcoming space. A TV wall with a dark stone panel and wooden detailing anchors the family seating area.

Staircase and lounge

A staircase with a screen of slender gold rods and a cluster of pendant lights forms a dramatic centrepiece, beside a lounge with a curved blue sofa and a marble-clad wall.

Pooja room and dining

A dedicated pooja space is designed in white and gold with decorative Om motifs, a peacock-inspired backdrop and a jaali screen, creating a calm, devotional corner. Nearby, the dining area pairs a dark stone table with burgundy upholstered chairs.

Bedrooms

The bedrooms are plush and restful, with tufted upholstered beds, floral backlit wall panels framed in wood and gold, and drum-shaped bedside tables. Soft rugs, layered bedding and warm bedside lamps complete the look.

Façade

The exterior is designed in a classic style with steep gabled roofs, decorative mouldings and warm lighting that highlights every level, giving the home a grand, timeless presence.

Design approach

The design embraces glamour while keeping the home comfortable. Gold, crystal and polished stone are balanced with soft fabrics, warm lighting and a calm cream base, and the pooja room gives the family a peaceful space at the heart of the house.

How Our Interior Design and Architecture Process Works

  1. Consultation: a free first meeting to understand your space, goals, budget and timeline.
  2. Brief and survey: accurate measurements and a written brief that guides the design.
  3. Concept and planning: layouts, mood boards and material directions.
  4. 3D visualisation: photorealistic views and walkthroughs, refined with you.
  5. Working drawings: detailed drawings coordinated across structure, services and interiors.
  6. Procurement and execution: supervised work on site to a fixed timeline.
  7. Handover: a finished space backed by our 10-year warranty.

Why Combine Interior Design and Architecture?

When the building and its interiors are planned together, windows land where furniture needs light, beams avoid wardrobe walls and electrical points match the final layout. Combining interior design and architecture also means one point of accountability, one schedule and one budget. It is simpler for you and produces a more coherent result.

Choosing the Right Service

Not sure where to start? These questions usually point the way:

  • Is the building already built? If yes, start with interior design. If not, bring in our architects first so interior design and architecture develop together.
  • Is it a home or a business? Homes are planned around family routines. Businesses are planned around customers, staff and brand.
  • How much of the space is changing? A single room, a full renovation or a new build each need a different scope.
  • What is your timeline? A fixed move-in or opening date shapes how the project is phased.

At the first consultation, our team will recommend the right combination of services for your project and explain clearly what each stage involves and what it will cost.

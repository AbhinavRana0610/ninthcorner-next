What Our Interior Design Services Include

Most people come to us with a feeling rather than a brief: the flat feels cramped, the office no longer suits the team, the new villa is a shell that needs a personality. Our interior design services turn that feeling into a plan you can build. Over 22 years and more than 800 completed spaces, we have learned that the order of decisions matters as much as the decisions themselves, so every project follows the same disciplined sequence.

1. Brief and site study

We start by measuring the space and listening. Who uses each room, at what time of day, and for what? Where does the light come from, and where does storage keep running out? The answers become a written brief that the whole team designs against.

2. Concept and space planning

Layouts come before finishes. We test furniture positions, circulation routes and door swings on plan, then present a concept with mood boards so you can see the direction before any money is committed.

3. 3D visualisation

Photorealistic 3D views and walkthroughs let you stand inside the design before it exists. This is where most changes happen, and changing a drawing costs far less than changing a wall.

4. Working drawings and material selection

Once the design is approved, we prepare detailed drawings for joinery, false ceilings, electrical points, lighting and flooring, and finalise every material and finish with you.

5. Procurement, execution and handover

Our project managers coordinate vendors, site teams and quality checks against structured milestones. We stay on site through installation and hand over a finished space, backed by our 10-year warranty and fixed timelines.

How We Keep Projects on Budget

Budget overruns usually come from late decisions. Because drawings, materials and quantities are fixed before work starts, our pricing is transparent and milestone-based, with no hidden costs added halfway through. If the budget needs to come down, we show you where a change saves money without weakening the design, rather than quietly swapping in cheaper materials.

Questions Worth Asking Any Designer

  • Will I see the design in 3D before work starts on site?
  • Is the quote broken into milestones, and what exactly does each one cover?
  • Who supervises the site day to day, and how often will I get updates?
  • What warranty applies to the finished work, and for how long?

Clear answers to these questions are a good sign that a firm will deliver what it draws.

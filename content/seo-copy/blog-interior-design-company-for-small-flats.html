Room-by-Room Ideas for Small Flats

Living room

Choose a sofa scaled to the room, with raised legs so the floor stays visible. A wall-mounted TV unit with closed storage below keeps surfaces clear, and a single large rug makes the room feel more generous than several small ones.

Kitchen

In small flats, the kitchen often needs to do more than cook. Tall units, corner carousels, pull-out pantries and a breakfast counter that doubles as a work surface all help. Light-coloured shutters and under-cabinet lighting make a compact kitchen feel brighter.

Bedroom

A hydraulic storage bed, floor-to-ceiling sliding wardrobes and wall-mounted bedside shelves free up floor space. Keep the palette soft and the décor minimal for a calm retreat.

Bathroom

Wall-hung toilets and vanities, a glass shower partition and recessed niches for toiletries make even a tight bathroom feel open and easy to clean.

Balcony

Many small flats have a balcony that ends up as storage. With foldable seating, a few plants and good lighting, it can become an extra living area.

Mistakes to Avoid in Small Flats

  • Oversized furniture: large sofas and beds make rooms feel cramped and block circulation.
  • Too many colours: a busy palette breaks the space visually and makes it feel smaller.
  • Open shelving everywhere: visible clutter quickly overwhelms compact rooms.
  • Ignoring lighting: one ceiling light leaves corners dark and shrinks the room.

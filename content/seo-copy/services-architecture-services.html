How Our Architecture Services Work

A building is the one part of a project you cannot easily change later. That is why our architecture services put most of the effort into the early stages, when moving a wall or turning a staircase costs nothing more than a revised drawing. Over 22 years and more than 800 completed spaces, our architects and interior designers have refined a sequence that keeps decisions in the right order.

Site analysis and brief

We study the plot, its orientation, the local building rules, access, neighbouring structures and the way sun and wind move across the site. Alongside this, we write a brief with you: how many people will use the building, what each floor needs to hold, and how the property should grow over the next decade.

Concept design

Massing studies and plan options show different ways the brief could fit on the site. We compare them on light, privacy, circulation, cost and how well they connect indoors and outdoors, and then develop the strongest option with you.

3D visualisation

Photorealistic 3D views and walkthroughs make the design easy to understand before construction. Elevations, façade materials and interior volumes can all be reviewed and adjusted while change is still cheap.

Working drawings and coordination

Our architecture services include the detailed drawings a contractor builds from, coordinated with structural, electrical and plumbing requirements so that beams, ducts and points do not clash on site.

Site supervision

During construction, we visit the site at key stages to check that the work matches the drawings and to resolve questions quickly, so small issues do not become expensive rework.

Architecture and Interiors Under One Roof

Many problems in finished buildings come from architects and interior designers working separately. A column lands in the middle of a wardrobe wall, or a window sits where the bed needs to go. Because our team handles both, interior layouts are considered while the building is still being drawn. Ceiling heights, window positions and electrical points are planned around the furniture you will actually use. If interiors are your main concern, our interior design services page explains that side of the work.

Designing for Comfort and Long Life

Good architecture keeps working long after handover. We orient rooms to bring in daylight while limiting harsh afternoon sun, plan cross-ventilation where the site allows, and choose durable façade and roofing materials that age well. These choices lower running costs and make a building more pleasant to live or work in every day. Where clients want it, Vastu principles are considered alongside planning and structure, not added as an afterthought.
